## ✈️ Project Overview

**Travel Smart at the Airport** is a Generative AI-powered passenger-awareness campaign designed to encourage domestic airline passengers to use reliable and verified travel information before travelling.

The project focuses on using Generative AI to create professional passenger-awareness content while maintaining **accuracy, privacy, compliance, source verification, and human review**.

The campaign addresses the problem of passengers relying on incomplete or unverified travel information, particularly from social media.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Encourage passengers to check official airline and airport information.
- Create clear and professional passenger-awareness content.
- Use Generative AI responsibly for aviation communication.
- Reduce unsupported or misleading claims.
- Maintain human review and approval throughout the workflow.
- Protect passenger privacy and confidential information.

## 🔍 Review and Verification

All generated content was reviewed for:

- Accuracy
- Tone
- Compliance
- Context
- Privacy
- Source support
- Visual quality

Unsupported claims, invented baggage rules, fake statistics, misleading safety information, fake logos, and incorrect airport information were identified as risks and removed or marked for verification.

---

## 🔐 Privacy and Responsible AI

The project does **not** use real passenger personal information, booking references, passport details, passwords, credentials, or confidential operational information.

AI-generated information must be checked against appropriate official or approved sources before being used in passenger-facing communication.

---

## ⚠️ Key Risks Identified

- AI hallucinations
- Outdated information
- Incorrect policy claims
- Privacy exposure
- Misleading visuals
- AI-generated text errors
- Unsupported operational information

Human judgement remains responsible for final passenger communication and business decisions.
